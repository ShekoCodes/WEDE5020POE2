Our Founder & Royal Story

The Afro Crown Beauty was founded in 2024 by Nkahosheko Kopa, a young model and cultural ambassador from Mohlakeng, Randfontein. Nkahosheko gained international acclaim in December 2022 when she was crowned Miss Kids Star International in Thailand at just 16 years old. While representing South Africa on global stages, she discovered that true beauty begins with self acceptance, authentic identity and honoring one's natural heritage.

As a proud wearer of 4C natural hair, Nkahosheko personally understood the daily struggles of dryness, shrinkage and lack of specialised care for tightly coiled textures. Her deepest inspiration stemmed from her ancestral roots in the Eastern Cape, where her grandmother passed down rich traditions of using natural botanicals, pure oils and gentle scalp practices to protect and nourish African hair.

Combining her experience in international fashion with deep rooted African hair rituals, Nkahosheko created The Afro Crown Beauty to eliminate the frustration surrounding textured hair care. What began as a personal passion to care for her own 4C crown quickly blossomed into a premier boutique salon and organic product line dedicated to restoring pride in natural coils.

Our mission is to empower men, women and youth to step into their royalty by embracing their natural hair. Through gentle salon treatments, educational guidance on hair health, and pure nutrient dense products, we aim to ensure every client walks away feeling regal and confident.

Our vision is to become Africa’s leading natural hair authority inspiring a global movement where 4C coils, locs and rich natural textures are proudly worn and celebrated as the ultimate crown of beauty.

We operate on three core pillars:

  • Cultural & Ancestral Pride: Honoring Eastern Cape heritage and African beauty in every style.
  • 4C & Coil Integrity First: Prioritizing scalp health and moisture retention over harsh chemical quick fixes.
  • Pure Botanical Formulations: Clean, organic ingredients sourced to nourish coily textures naturally.
